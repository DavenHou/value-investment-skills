# Industry archetypes

Select the closest archetype for each material segment. Use disclosed company KPIs before generic sector proxies. If the company's economics do not fit an archetype, state the custom unit and why.

| Archetype | Revenue equation | Leading demand evidence | Margin and cycle checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing, equipment, components | Units shipped × realised price × mix | Customer production, orders, backlog, channel inventory | Input cost, utilisation, capacity, warranty, freight, discounting |
| Commodity, materials, energy | Sales volume × realised commodity price | Consumption, customer inventory, supply disruption | Benchmark price, grade/location spread, costs, hedging, industry capacity |
| Consumer, retail, restaurants | Stores/traffic × conversion × basket | Same-store sales, traffic, promotions, consumer income | Promotion intensity, mix, shrink, rent/labour, new-store maturity |
| Software, internet, platform | Customers/users × ARPU or take rate | Bookings, pipeline, active users, retention, usage | Churn, sales efficiency, cloud cost, creator/merchant incentives, competition |
| Telecom, utility, infrastructure | Customers or usage × tariff/rate | Connections, usage, regulated demand, contracted backlog | Regulation, allowed return, fuel/input pass-through, network utilisation, capex |
| Healthcare, pharma, medtech | Patient volume × price × adoption | Prescription, procedure, reimbursement, launch uptake | Patent loss, trial/regulatory outcome, rebate, salesforce and R&D spend |
| Financials | Earning assets × spread + fee base × fee rate | Loan/deposit growth, AUM, trading/customer activity | Funding cost, credit losses, capital, claims/loss ratio, market levels |
| Property, construction, infrastructure projects | Contracted sales/backlog × completion or milestone recognition | New contracts, presales, tender awards, permits, financing | Land/material/labour costs, delivery timing, working capital, cancellations |
| Transport and logistics | Volume/passengers × yield | Trade, industrial output, bookings, load factor | Fuel, network or fleet utilisation, wage cost, pricing, capacity additions |
| Professional and outsourced services | Billable headcount × utilisation × billing rate | Hiring, client pipeline, contract renewal | Wage inflation, subcontracting, attrition, utilisation, delivery mix |

## Segment and portfolio rules

- Forecast conglomerates from their business units. Do not apply one group growth rate when segment cycles differ.
- Forecast cyclicals using activity, price, inventory, and capacity separately; do not infer the cycle from one commodity price alone.
- Forecast regulated industries from approved rates, volumes, and timing of recovery mechanisms; policy headlines alone are insufficient.
- Forecast businesses with deferred revenue, construction progress, or long contracts by separating bookings, backlog, billings, cash collection, and revenue recognition.
- Forecast asset-heavy businesses with volume/utilisation and depreciation or fixed-cost absorption explicitly.
- Forecast financial businesses from their balance-sheet or fee base, not from a gross-margin template.
