# Competition, pricing, and cycles

Use this reference for cyclical, capacity-intensive, commodity-linked, or discount-prone industries.

## Diagnose the regime

| Regime | Observable evidence | Typical earnings transmission |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery / restocking | Orders or production improve before sales; inventories stop rising | Volume recovers first; utilisation and margin may lag |
| Expansion / tight supply | High utilisation, long lead times, falling inventory, firm prices | Volume and price improve; fixed-cost leverage supports margins |
| Peak | Capacity and capex accelerate while orders slow | Revenue can remain high while forward pricing weakens |
| Correction / destocking | Orders below shipments, inventory reduction, utilisation falls | Volume declines; fixed costs and discounting pressure margins |
| Overcapacity / price war | Supply additions exceed demand, weak utilisation, falling realised prices, competitor discounting | Unit price and margin fall even if units grow |

Do not label a market from a single signal. Seek at least two independent indicators and note conflicts.

## Price-war diagnostic

Test these explanations in order before attributing a revenue or margin change to a price war:

1. product, customer, channel, geography, or contract-timing mix;
2. FX translation or commodity pass-through;
3. normal contract reset or promotion;
4. share-gaining discounting by the company or a competitor;
5. broad industry overcapacity and persistent discounting.

Evidence consistent with a price war includes falling realised price after mix adjustment, falling industry utilisation, persistent gross-margin compression, aggressive capacity additions, and customer or competitor disclosures indicating discounts. State which evidence is direct and which is inferred.

## Quantify the transmission

For a material price or cost move, show a simple sensitivity:

```text
Revenue effect = volume × change in realised price
Gross-profit effect ≈ revenue effect − pass-through-adjusted cost effect
```

For fixed-cost-heavy operations, separately show utilisation or volume sensitivity. Do not assume lower input prices lift gross margin if customers receive the saving through contractual or competitive repricing.

## Forecast output

For each scenario, specify the regime, two to five changed drivers, a measurable confirmation signal, and an invalidation signal. Use a range when the timing of a cycle turn, contract reset, or capacity start-up is uncertain.
